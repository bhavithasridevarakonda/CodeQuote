About CodeQuote & The DevOps Workflow

A pure vanilla web application built to demonstrate an industrial CI/CD engineering lifecycle.

CodeQuote was designed with zero external runtime dependencies (no heavy frontend frameworks, no database engines, no external third-party APIs). This architectural purity guarantees instantaneous load times and deterministic behavior, making it the perfect foundation for learning, demonstrating, and defending a comprehensive DevOps pipeline during viva voce examinations.
